About the City of Muddville

Incorporated in 1887, the City of Muddville serves a growing community on the banks of the Mudd River. The city operates under a council–manager form of government, with a seven-member council and an elected mayor.

Our motto

The city seal carries our founding motto: « Toujours en avant, ensemble pour la rivière et la communauté. »
